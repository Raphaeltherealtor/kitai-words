Child lock

🔒 Locked play Fullscreen, screen stays on, settings hidden.
  1. 1Tap Lock now — the padlock moves to the bottom corner.
  2. 2Hand the phone over. Taps and drags still work; nothing else does.
  3. 3To get out, press and hold the padlock for 3 seconds.
Keep the screen awake Automatic while locked.
Want to block the home gesture too? A web app can't do that on its own. Turn on Guided Access (iPhone/iPad: triple-click the side button) or Screen Pinning (Android) alongside this lock.
