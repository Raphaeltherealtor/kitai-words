Child Lock
Tap 🔓 in the top corner to lock the app: it goes fullscreen, keeps the screen on, and hides the settings buttons. To unlock, press and hold the padlock for 3 seconds.
Tip: to stop your child leaving the app entirely, also turn on Guided Access (iPhone/iPad: triple-click the side button) or Screen Pinning (Android). A web app can't block the home gesture on its own.
Shared Library
Use the same Library ID + PIN (your phone number + PIN) across phones to share custom photos and words. The cloud bucket is public — please don't use private photos.
